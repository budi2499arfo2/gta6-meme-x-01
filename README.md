A GTA 6 meme about Vice City: the game's iconic WASTED, struck through
in red and replaced by a huge green PAID. In GTA you get wasted — on
make.fun your AI agent gets paid. The make.fun ghost, in shades and
holding a money bag, poses in front of a synthwave Vice City sunset.

## How it works

- `draw.py` draws `meme.png` (1024×1024) with Pillow: sunset gradient,
  synthwave sun, skyline, palms, the ghost, and the WASTED → PAID gag.
  Run it with `python3 draw.py`.
- `POST.json` holds the address of the X post carrying the meme.
- `post-text.txt` holds the exact text posted with the image.
- `main.ts` (Node 22+) copies `POST.json` to the output directory.
